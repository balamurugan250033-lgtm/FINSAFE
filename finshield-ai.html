Smart Intervention Queue

Customers with the highest financial distress scores are prioritized for early intervention. This system uses a Max Heap Priority Queue data structure for efficient customer prioritization.

📊 DSA Concept Explanation

Max Heap Priority Queue: This intervention queue uses a Max Heap data structure to maintain customers in priority order based on their financial distress score.

  • Insertion: O(log n) - New customers are inserted while maintaining heap property
  • Extraction: O(log n) - Highest priority (most distressed) customer always at the top
  • Prioritization: Risk score determines position; higher score = higher priority
  • Benefit: Bank can efficiently identify and assist customers who need help most urgently

In a production system, this queue would automatically update as new financial data arrives, ensuring the most critical customers always remain at the top for intervention.

⚖️ Responsible AI & Ethical Financial Intelligence

✓ Early Warning System: FinShield AI provides early warnings and risk indicators, supporting human decision-making rather than making automated judgments about customers.
✓ Risk as Support Tool: Risk scores should support and inform intervention strategies, not automatically deny loans, banking services, or credit access.
✓ Transparency & Explainability: Every recommendation is transparent and clearly explains the financial factors driving the risk assessment.
✓ Data Confidentiality: Customer financial data is treated as highly confidential and accessed only by authorized personnel for legitimate intervention purposes.
✓ Customer Rights: Customers have the right to understand, review, and challenge any risk assessment or recommendation affecting their financial services.
✓ Support-Based Language: The system uses supportive, non-judgmental language (e.g., "offer support," "review situation," "provide assistance") rather than punitive messaging.
